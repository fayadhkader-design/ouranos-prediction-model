# Ouranos

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Purpose
An experimental spacecraft telemetry workbench. The operator compares measured early warnings against conventional alarms and inspects the evidence behind each score.

## Design brief
The requested dark mission-control dashboard uses restrained cyan, readable technical typography, and no decorative science-fiction effects. The operator reviews evolving telemetry at a desk; a low-glare dark surface keeps long charts comfortable. Scientific modesty and visible simulated-data provenance take priority over dramatic claims.
